Maintenance plan terms

What Counts as a Small Update?

  • Changing a phone number
  • Updating an address
  • Changing business hours
  • Replacing an image
  • Editing a few lines of text
  • Adding or removing an existing service
  • Fixing minor display issues

What Is Not Included?

  • New website pages
  • Complete website redesigns
  • E-commerce functionality
  • New booking systems
  • Custom coding
  • Major SEO campaigns
  • Large amounts of new content

  • Third-party subscriptions or services
  • Major functionality changes

Services and changes outside the selected maintenance package may be quoted separately.
