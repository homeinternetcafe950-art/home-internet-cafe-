About Us

Your neighbourhood tech stop

Practical technology, printing, CV and online application assistance — the kind of help you'd normally have to travel across town for.

What We Do

One counter for the tasks that pile up

Home Internet Cafe provides computer support — Windows and Office installation and troubleshooting — alongside printing and copying, CV typing, help completing online applications, and website development and maintenance. Whatever the task, it's handled in one visit, or one WhatsApp message.

We keep the process simple: tell us what you need, we quote you a fixed price upfront, and we get it done the same day wherever possible.

“Professional, friendly service.”
Who We Serve

Individuals and small businesses in Temba

We work with job seekers who need a CV typed or an application completed, and with small businesses in Temba, Hammanskraal and surrounding areas that need computer support, printing, or a website built and kept running. Our physical services — printing, scanning, computer diagnostics and repairs — are based at 950 Hans Kekana View. Website development and maintenance can be arranged remotely for clients further afield.
